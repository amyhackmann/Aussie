How to play

Daily Mode

Empty the board. Ranked by fewest cats left, then time, then most cats cleared, then most tiles never played.

Drag pieces from the tray onto the grid.
Three or more of a colour clear when they touch side to side — corners don't count. A full row or column clears too, and both can happen on one move.
It ends when the board is empty, daily tiles run out or nothing fits.
Every 30 cats cleared adds a fortune cookie (clears 3×3) or a red packet (clears its row and column) to your deck. Drag it anywhere, even on top of cats.
Unlimited Cat Mode

Collect as many cats as you can. Ranked by most cats, with ties going to the faster run.

∞The cats never run out. It ends when nothing fits.

About Lucky Streak

Life is made of little moments worth noticing.

A good game. A curious discovery. A ridiculous cat. A perfect little streak of things going right.

Lucky Streak is a celebration of those moments — and a reminder to be grateful for them.

Play, notice, enjoy.

There's a lot of good hiding in the little things.
